Introduction

Welcome to Code Editor Land.

Code Editor Land, operated by PlayForm in Sofia, Bulgaria ("us", "we", or "our"), operates https://land.playform.cloud, the Land desktop editor, related APIs, account systems, synchronization features, documentation, downloads, and associated services (collectively, the "Service").

This Privacy Policy governs your use of our Service and explains how we collect, safeguard, use, and disclose information that results from your use of the Service.

We use your data to provide and improve Service. By using Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. Unless otherwise defined in this Privacy Policy, the terms used in this Privacy Policy have the same meanings as in our Terms of Service.

Our Terms of Service govern all use of our Service and together with this Privacy Policy constitute your agreement with us.

Definitions

SERVICE means the https://land.playform.cloud website, the Code Editor Land desktop application, and related services operated by PlayForm for the Code Editor Land project.

PERSONAL DATA means data about a living individual who can be identified from those data, or from those data together with other information either in our possession or likely to come into our possession.

USAGE DATA is data collected automatically either generated by the use of Service or from Service infrastructure itself, such as page visits, feature usage, performance signals, or diagnostic information.

COOKIES are small files stored on your device that help remember preferences, maintain sessions, or support analytics and security functions.

DATA CONTROLLER means the natural or legal person who determines the purposes for which and the manner in which any personal data are processed. For the purpose of this Privacy Policy, we are the Data Controller of your data.

DATA PROCESSORS (OR SERVICE PROVIDERS) means any natural or legal person who processes the data on behalf of the Data Controller. We may use the services of various Service Providers in order to process your data more effectively.

DATA SUBJECT is any living individual who is the subject of Personal Data.

USER means the individual using our Service. The User corresponds to the Data Subject, who is the subject of Personal Data.

Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide, maintain, secure, and improve our Service to you.

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"). Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  1. Email address.
  2. Username, display name, and account profile information.
  3. Authentication details and linked identity provider data.
  4. Communications you send to us, including support messages, legal notices, feedback, and bug reports.
  5. Optional profile details such as avatar, biography, or other information you choose to provide.

When you sign in using a social identity provider (GitHub, Google, GitLab, Okta, SAML, or Azure AD), the sign-in flow is handled by Auth0 (see Service Providers below). Auth0 acts as the OAuth intermediary: it receives your identity data from the third-party provider and returns a standardized profile to us. The data we receive typically includes your email address, display name, and avatar URL provided by the identity source. We do not receive or request access to private repositories, private files, or write-access scopes unless we separately and explicitly disclose such a request.

We may use your Personal Data to contact you with service notices, security alerts, account information, newsletters, marketing or promotional materials, and other information that may be of interest to you. You may opt out of receiving any, or all, of these communications from us by following the unsubscribe link or by using the Withdraw Consent form (income code LEAVE) or emailing support@land.playform.cloud.

Usage Data

We may also collect information that your browser, device, or application sends whenever you visit our Service or when you access Service by or through a desktop or mobile device ("Usage Data").

This Usage Data may include information such as your Internet Protocol address (for example, IP address), browser type, browser version, operating system, device type, language settings, the pages of our Service that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers, crash diagnostics, performance information, feature usage, and other diagnostic data.

When you access Service with the desktop application or another device, this Usage Data may include information such as device type, device unique ID, IP address, operating system, app version, extension activity, startup performance, memory usage, error logs, and other diagnostic data related to Service operation.

Tracking Data

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track the activity on our Service and hold certain information.

Tracking technologies used may include cookies, local storage, beacons, tags, scripts, or similar technologies to collect and track information and to improve and analyze our Service.

We may use essential cookies for authentication and security, preference cookies to remember settings such as theme or editor preferences, and analytics cookies or similar technologies to understand how the Service is used.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept some cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our Service.

Retention of Data

We will retain your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will also retain Usage Data for internal analysis purposes. Where technically feasible, we may delete, aggregate, or anonymize data once it is no longer needed for identifiable processing purposes.

Our infrastructure and service providers may process data in multiple jurisdictions, including the European Union, the United States, and other countries where our providers operate.

  4. the right of restriction. You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal information;
  5. the right to data portability. You have the right to be provided with a copy of your Personal Data in a structured, machine-readable, and commonly used format;
  6. the right to withdraw consent. You also have the right to withdraw your consent at any time where we rely on your consent to process your personal information.

Please note that we may ask you to verify your identity before responding to such requests. Please also note that we may not be able to provide some portions of Service without certain necessary data.

You have the right to complain to a data protection authority about our collection and use of your Personal Data. For more information, please contact your local data protection authority.

According to CalOPPA, we agree to the following:

  1. users can visit our public website pages anonymously where account access is not required;
  2. our Privacy Policy link includes the word "Privacy", and can easily be found on the page specified above on the home page of our website;
  3. users will be notified of any privacy policy changes on this Privacy Policy page;
  4. users are able to change or request correction of personal information using the Data Correction form (AMEND) or by emailing privacy@land.playform.cloud.

Our Policy on "Do Not Track" Signals:

Some browsers incorporate a Do Not Track feature that signals to websites that you visit that you do not want your online activity tracked. Because there is not yet a common understanding of how to interpret Do Not Track signals, our Service may not respond uniformly to all such signals.

Service Providers

We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Service ("Service Providers"), provide Service on our behalf, perform Service-related services, or assist us in analyzing how our Service is used.

These third parties may have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose, subject to their own legal and contractual obligations.

The following third-party service providers process personal data on our behalf as part of operating the Service. Each provider is bound by its own privacy policy and data processing terms.

Auth0 (by Okta) - Authentication and identity management
Auth0 powers all user authentication on the Service, including sign-in, sign-up, session management, and identity federation with third-party providers (GitHub, Google, GitLab, Okta Workforce, SAML 2.0, and Azure AD). When you create an account or sign in, Auth0 processes your email address, display name, profile picture, and linked identity data. Our Auth0 tenant is hosted in the European Union (eu.auth0.com). Auth0 uses PKCE-based OAuth 2.0 - no client secret is exposed to the browser. Auth0 may set session cookies or tokens in your browser to maintain your authenticated state. Auth0 privacy policy: okta.com/privacy-policy.

Cloudflare - Infrastructure, CDN, and edge APIs
The Service is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Backend APIs (account management, downloads, status, analytics ingestion) run as Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare may process your IP address, request headers, geographic region, and usage metadata for routing, security, and DDoS mitigation. Cloudflare privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

PostHog - Product analytics
We use PostHog to collect anonymized usage analytics including page views, feature interactions, and performance signals. PostHog does not receive your name or email address through this integration. Data collected via PostHog is used solely to improve the Service. PostHog privacy policy: posthog.com/privacy.

GitHub - Source code and issue tracking
Our source code and issue tracker are hosted on GitHub. If you submit an issue or pull request on GitHub, that interaction is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.
