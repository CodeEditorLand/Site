/THE INTERVALS

Four distances, and no fifth

Every band in this document opens at one of four intervals. They are a ladder at about 1.4 times a rung - the smallest step the eye reads as a different distance - two wide, two tight, which is what lets a page say "wide" twice without saying it at the same volume.

THE LADDER, 04 # THE LADDER / 04

REST:
56PX TIGHT the band continues the subject above it 24 ABOVE, 32 BELOW
BEAT:
80PX TIGHT a sibling subject, and what a band that declares nothing gets 48 ABOVE, 32 BELOW
BREAK:
112PX WIDE a new movement, and no rule 80 ABOVE, 32 BELOW
TURN:
168PX WIDE a chapter, and the block prints its boundary rule 136 ABOVE, 32 BELOW

THE LAW, 05 # THE LAW / 05

A BAND DECLARES ITS INTERVAL:
IN ITS DATA, ONCE `Band.interval`, printed as `data-interval` by the block. A band that declares nothing is a beat - the interval is an enhancement, never a requirement.
TWO ADJACENT BANDS:
NEVER THE SAME RUNG They may share a register - wide, wide, tight, tight, wide is a shape - but the second rung inside a register is what keeps two wide bands at two volumes. The layer steps a repeat up the ladder, so a page that repeats itself accelerates into a break.
A CHAPTER IS A TURN:
THE BLOCK PRINTS THE RULE `turn` adds `record-band--chapter`, so the boundary rule cannot appear on a band that is not a chapter, or be forgotten by one that is.
THE PAGE'S FIRST AND LAST BAND:
NO RULE, THEN A SETTLING The header already ends the page, so the first band paints no boundary; the last closes at the rest interval, because a page decelerates rather than stopping.
INSIDE AN ARTIFACT:
THE TIGHT REGISTER ONLY A part of a subject is never a chapter of it: a legal document's clauses alternate rest and beat at the atomic steps, 16 and 32.

THE ARMS, 09 # THE ARMS / 09

EMPTY:
A PAGE WITH ONE BAND It is both the first and the last, so it paints no boundary and closes at the rest interval. A page with one subject is not a chapter of itself.
LOADING:
THE SAME BODY SLOT A skeleton sits where the facts will sit, at the same interval, so the seam does not move when they arrive. No spinner: the block draws the schema.
PARTIAL:
NO HOLE A band with no standfirst or no index strip leaves no gap, because every part of the head carries its own distance rather than the head carrying one gap for all of them.
ERROR:
SPACED LIKE ANY BODY A failed action is a `RecordFailure` in the body and the band does not react to it. A rhythm that changed for a failure would be emphasising the failure.
STALE:
UNCHANGED An as-of is a fact inside the block. The band is the same distance from its neighbour whether the figure under it is an hour old or a year.
TOO MUCH:
THE GUARD STEPS THE REPEAT UP Two identical rungs in a row and the second becomes the next rung; a third becomes the one after. A run of four is a miscomposition and this register prints it rather than hiding it.
TOO LONG:
THE SEAM DOES NOT MOVE The interval is padding on the box, so a heading that wraps four times takes more room inside its band and never changes the distance between two bands.
MISSING:
NO STRIP, NO GAP A band whose index strip has no cells prints no strip - never an empty one - and its body's distance is unchanged.
DENIED:
NOT A BAND A surface the reader may not see is a `ShellState` in the shell's own band. The page's sequence does not include a band that is not printed, and the page closes on its own last band.

/THE GRAMMAR

Eight marks, and no others

Every page of this document is printed in the same eight marks. They are not decoration: each one carries a relation a sentence would need a clause to say.

#
A series marker: which record this is, and which set it belongs to.
〈 〉
Containment: the runtime claim, or the stamp a record is filed under.
/
A route, a command or a category - one slash, never a second.
→
Direction: what a step leads to.
↳
Relation: the one-line proposition a record hangs from its nameplate.
:
A field label - `STATUS:`, `TARGET:`, `LICENSE:` - the colon is the label's.
◎
The fallback: a value that is not there, printed rather than left blank.
U+2001
The only separator. An em quad, never a middle dot.

The grammar is level one - no card, no page and no later decision overrules it. The marks are written as literals in the source, never as characters an editor could silently fold into a middle dot.

/THE LANGUAGES

Four ways to compose a page

A page is not a stack of bands. It is read, operated, audited or shown, and each of those is a different set of tracks: a reading page is given RAIL MEASURE MARGIN, a workspace page RAIL PANES DRAWER, an audit page LEDGER MATRIX WELLS, and a plate page PLATE CHANNEL HEAD.

A page declares its language by being filed in `Source/Component/Shell/Language.ts`, and nowhere else: `Base.astro` writes the answer on `<html>` for the running head, `PageShell` reads the same answer for its grid, and the page prints the declaration strip above its first track. ENTRIES is counted from that register; a prefix entry covers every route under it.
